    Data and method

    The unemployment rate (U-3), U-6, employment from the household survey and the group rates come from the Bureau of Labor Statistics via FRED, seasonally adjusted (by the BLS where it publishes an adjusted series, otherwise with STL). Family-income deciles come from IPUMS CPS microdata. Recession dates, the committee’s measures and its words on method come from the NBER’s Business Cycle Dating Committee. Two versions of the rule are used. The record since 1950 and the grid of groups use the Sahm Rule as it is usually computed: a series’ three-month average less its lowest three-month average over the previous twelve months, signaling at half a point. The chart of who is affected first and the racial, educational and income turning points use the website’s version, on its CPS series: a six-month average less its lowest six-month average of the last twelve months, this month included, with the alarm at half a point. Readings use today’s revised data, not the data available at the time.
